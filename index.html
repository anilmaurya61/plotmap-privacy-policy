Privacy Policy

PlotMap UP – Khasra & Khata

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

PlotMap UP – Khasra & Khata is an independent informational application that helps users access publicly available land map and land record information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, process, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from users.

The application does not require user registration, login, or account creation.

Permissions Used

  • Internet Permission – Required to load publicly available land map and record information.
  • No sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, or Location are collected or stored.

Data Sources

The information displayed in this application is obtained from publicly available government sources, including:

  • https://upbhunaksha.gov.in
  • https://upbhulekh.gov.in

Users are encouraged to verify information directly from the official government websites for legal or official purposes.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information, no user data is retained on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The application may use standard platform services such as Google Play Services required for app distribution and functionality. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Disclaimer

Important: This application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with any government entity, department, or agency.

PlotMap UP – Khasra & Khata is an independent application that provides access to publicly available information for informational and convenience purposes only.

This application does not represent any government authority and does not provide official land ownership verification, certified records, legal advice, or legal documentation.

For official records, legal verification, and authoritative information, users should refer directly to the respective government departments and official websites.
