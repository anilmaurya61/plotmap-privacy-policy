Privacy Policy

UP Bhunaksha – Khasra Naksha

Last Updated: July 2026

Introduction

UP Bhunaksha – Khasra Naksha is an independent informational application that allows users to access publicly available Uttar Pradesh land map (Bhunaksha), Khasra, and land record information from official government websites.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, process, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

The application does not require user registration, login, or account creation.

Permissions Used

  • Internet Permission – Required only to load publicly available land map and land record information.
  • The application does not request or access Camera, Microphone, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, Storage, or Location permissions.

Data Sources

The information displayed within this application is obtained from publicly available government websites:

  • https://upbhunaksha.gov.in
  • https://upbhulekh.gov.in

Users should verify all information directly from the official government websites before using it for legal or official purposes.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information, no user data is stored on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The application may use standard Google Play Services required for app distribution and functionality. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Disclaimer

Important:

This application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with any Government entity, department, or agency.

UP Bhunaksha – Khasra Naksha is an independent application that provides convenient access to publicly available land map and land record information.

This application does not represent any government authority and does not provide official land ownership verification, certified records, legal advice, or legal documentation.

For official records, legal verification, and authoritative information, users should visit the respective official government websites.
